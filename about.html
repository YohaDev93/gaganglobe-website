About GGC

Industry insight. People-first recruitment.

Gagan Global Consulting Pvt. Ltd. is a professional manpower and staffing solutions company dedicated to connecting skilled talent with the evolving needs of businesses across diverse industries. We focus on reliable, industry-specific recruitment solutions built on quality, transparency and long-term partnerships.

Backed by engineering experience

Our parent company, Chaitanya Enterprises Engineering (I) Pvt. Ltd., brings more than 20 years of experience in electromechanical engineering, installation, commissioning, maintenance and turnkey infrastructure projects. Its engineering background helps inform our understanding of technical roles and project workforce requirements.

By combining sector knowledge with specialized recruitment capabilities, GGC aims to support operational excellence, project efficiency and sustainable business growth.
