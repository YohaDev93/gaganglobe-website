Privacy and transparency

Our approach to your personal information

Gagan Global Consulting Pvt Ltd ("GGC", "we", "our" or "us") provides recruitment, staffing and consulting-related information on gaganglobe.com. This Privacy Policy explains the information involved when you contact us or interact with our website.

1. Information you provide

When you use our enquiry form, the information you submit may include your full name, company or organization, email address, optional telephone number, type of enquiry, role or requirement, and the contents of your message. If you contact us by email, we receive the information you include in that email.

If you voluntarily share professional information for a career enquiry, such as qualifications or experience, we may use it to respond to that enquiry. Please do not send identity documents, financial details, medical information or other sensitive information through the general contact form unless specifically requested through an appropriate secure channel.

2. Why we use the information

We use the information you provide to answer enquiries, discuss recruitment or staffing requirements, respond to career or partnership requests, follow up on communications, and maintain relevant business correspondence. We may also process information when necessary to protect our website, comply with applicable law or address misuse.

3. How enquiries are delivered

Our website's contact form uses the third-party service FormSubmit to deliver enquiry messages to GGC's designated email addresses. By submitting the form, you send the entered information through that service for delivery to our team. Its handling of submissions is also governed by its privacy information.

Depending on the services configured for the site, website hosting, email delivery, font delivery and basic security systems may also process limited technical information such as a browser request or IP address. We do not present these third parties as GGC employees or directly control their independent privacy practices.

4. Sharing and disclosure

Enquiry details may be accessed by authorized GGC personnel and the service providers required to operate the website, transmit messages or maintain business communications. Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law. We do not intend to publish an individual's enquiry or personal contact details on the website.

5. Retention and security

GGC aims to keep enquiry information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the relevant business or recruitment purpose, subject to applicable legal obligations. Retention may depend on the nature of the enquiry and internal records requirements. We aim to use reasonable organizational and technical safeguards; however, no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices and privacy requests

You may ask GGC about personal information you have provided and request correction, updating, withdrawal of consent where applicable, or deletion, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements. To make a request, email ops@gaganglobe.com and describe your request. We may need to verify that the request relates to your information before acting on it.

7. Cookies and external links

The website may use technical resources supplied by its hosting provider or third-party services. If analytics, advertising tags or optional cookies are introduced later, GGC should update this notice and provide any required choice or consent mechanism. Links to external websites are subject to those sites' own privacy practices.

8. Children's information

This corporate recruitment and consulting website is not directed at children. Please do not submit a child's personal information through the website's general enquiry form.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services, website features or applicable requirements change. The latest version will appear on this page with an updated date.
